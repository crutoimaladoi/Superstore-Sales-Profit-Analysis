# Superstore-Sales-Profit-Analysis


About the project

In this project, I am analyzing the particular Superstore retail dataset to gain business insights for the supervisor.

The main goal is to gain business insights from a raw transactional dataset to help make smarter pricing, discount, and product recommendations.

Business Problem

Identifying why certain products are losing money despite making sales
How does discount affect the profitability?
Identifying best customers and best regions
Seasonal sales performance analysis



Dataset details
Source: Superstore Dataset 
Type: Transactional retail data
Row count: ~10,000+ 
Some of the columns:
Order Date
Sales
Profit
Discount
Category
Sub-Category
Region
Customer Name
Product Name
Quantity

What i used for this project
Python 
Pandas 
Matplotlib 
Seaborn
PyCharm


I cleaned the dataset by:
Removing duplicate records
Convert date columns to datetime format
Removing any null data
Fixing data type

 Data Analysis
1. Sales Performance Analysis
Calculated total sales and profit generated
Sales distribution across regions and categories
2. Customer Analysis

Identified top customers by total sales and profit
Analyzed repeat customer behavior
3. Product Analysis
Top selling products by quantity
Least profitable and loss-making products
sub-category performance


4. Time  Analysis
Monthly sales trends
Seasonal patterns in revenue


5. Profitability Analysis
Discount impact on profit
Negative-profit transaction detection
Performance by region
Takeaways


Positive correlation exists between higher discounts and lower profit
Technology category yields highest profit
Some sub-categories generate losses despite high sales
Some regions are underperforming in terms of profit
Small percentage of customers pad most of the revenue
